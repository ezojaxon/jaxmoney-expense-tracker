About JAXMONEY

A smarter way to manage your money.

JAXMONEY.

Your money. Your control. Your future.

VERSION 3.0

JAXMONEY is a personal finance management application designed to help you track income, expenses and financial activity.

Features:
• Income and expense tracking
• Multi-currency display
• Financial analytics
• Transaction history
• CSV reports
• Backup and restore
• Personalisation settings
• Dark mode

Storage: Your records are saved locally in your browser. This application does not automatically synchronise records across devices.
